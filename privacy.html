Privacy Policy — Young Cookiie Uploader

Last updated: November 9, 2026

Young Cookiie Uploader ("the app") is a personal tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel Young Cookiie to manage that channel's own videos.

YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using the app you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy).

Information the app accesses

Through Google sign-in (OAuth 2.0), the app accesses the YouTube account of the channel owner only in order to: upload videos, set or update their title, description, tags, privacy status and scheduled publish time, and read basic channel and video information to confirm uploads.

How information is used, stored and shared

  • Information is used only to perform the actions above for the owner's own channel.
  • OAuth tokens are stored only on the owner's computer. The app has no servers, and no YouTube data is stored anywhere else.
  • No data is sold, shared with or transferred to third parties. No cookies or tracking are used.

Data deletion and revoking access

  • You can revoke the app's access at any time from your Google security settings: https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Deleting the local token file removes all stored authorization data immediately.
  • Any stored API data is deleted or refreshed within 30 days, as required by the YouTube API Services Developer Policies. You can also request deletion by email (below) and it will be completed within 7 days.
